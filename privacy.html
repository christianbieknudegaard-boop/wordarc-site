Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

In short: Wordarc collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your games, stats and settings stay on your iPhone. Online play goes through Apple's Game Center, and purchases through the App Store.

Who we are

Wordarc is a word game for iPhone. In this policy “we” means the developer of Wordarc. You can reach us at hello@wordarc.app.

Data we collect

None. Wordarc has no user accounts and no servers of its own that receive your data. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Stored on your iPhone

Your settings, statistics, game history, XP and unlocks, Daily Arc results, progress in the Climb and any unfinished game against the bot are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are removed if you delete the app.

Online games (Game Center)

When you play online, Wordarc uses Apple's Game Center to find opponents, invite friends, pass turns and send turn notifications.

  • Your opponent sees your Game Center name and avatar, and the words played in your shared game.
  • The state of an online game is stored by Apple so both players can take turns. We never see it.
  • Signing in to Game Center is handled by iOS. Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center.

Word definitions

When you tap a word to see what it means, Wordarc looks the word up online: English words at Wiktionary (wiktionary.org) and Norwegian words at Ordbøkene (ord.uib.no, run by the University of Bergen and the Language Council of Norway). Only the word itself is sent. No information about you or your device is added by Wordarc, but like any web request, these services can see your IP address. Nothing is looked up unless you tap a word.

Notifications

If you turn on the Daily Arc reminder, Wordarc schedules a local notification on your iPhone. No push service or server of ours is involved. Notifications about online turns are sent by Game Center.

Purchases

Wordarc Plus is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email address or payment details. Wordarc only checks with Apple, on your device, whether Plus is active. Apple's privacy policy applies to these transactions.

Children

Wordarc does not collect data from anyone, including children. Online play uses Game Center, where Apple's parental controls (Screen Time) can limit multiplayer games and friend requests.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove everything Wordarc stores by deleting the app. Game Center data is managed in your Apple Account. You are welcome to contact us with any questions about privacy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change ever means data about you leaves your device, we'll ask for your permission in the app first.
