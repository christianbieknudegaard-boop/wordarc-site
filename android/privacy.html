Privacy Policy – Android

Effective 26 November 2026 · For Wordarc on Google Play. Using an iPhone? See the iPhone policy.

In short: Wordarc for Android collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your games, progress and settings stay on your phone. Purchases go through Google Play.

Who we are

Wordarc is a word game for Android and iPhone. In this policy “we” means the developer of Wordarc. You can reach us at hello@wordarc.app.

Data we collect

None. Wordarc has no user accounts and no servers of its own that receive your data. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites. Wordarc for Android does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Stored on your phone

Your settings, Daily Arc results, progress and stars in the Climb, and any unfinished game against the bot are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are removed if you uninstall the app.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under your Google account, and we cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

Wordarc Plus is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google through Google Play. We never see your name, email address or payment details. Wordarc only asks Google Play, on your phone, whether Plus has been bought on your Google account. Google's privacy policy applies to these transactions.

Permissions

  • Vibration, for haptic feedback while you play. You can turn it off in Wordarc's settings.
  • Google Play billing and network access, used only by Google Play's billing library to show the price of Plus and complete or restore a purchase.

Online play

Online play is not available in Wordarc for Android yet. Before it is added, we will update this policy to describe what it needs.

Children

Wordarc does not collect data from anyone, including children. Purchases can be limited with parental controls in Google Play and Google Family Link.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove everything Wordarc stores by uninstalling the app. Purchases are managed in your Google account. You are welcome to contact us with any questions about privacy, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in Norway: Datatilsynet).

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change ever means data about you leaves your phone, we'll ask for your permission in the app first.
