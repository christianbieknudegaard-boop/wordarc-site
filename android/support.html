Playing

How do I win?

Build a bridge of tiles from your foot of the arc to the crown at the top. Reach the top and hold it for one round. If your opponent can't knock you off in their turn, you win.

Which letters can I use?

A word must start next to your bridge, and each letter must touch the one before it. Letters already in your bridge can be reused. Through the fog you only see letters close to your tiles, so longer words open up more of the board.

How do I steal tiles?

A word of six letters or more may pass through your opponent's tiles and take them over. Shorter words can't go through their tiles.

What is a storm?

Play a word of seven letters or more and it sends a storm at your opponent: their highest unlocked tile is knocked loose, and the free letters along their bridge are shuffled. Each player can send two storms per game.

A word I know wasn't accepted

Wordarc uses large word lists for each language, but no list is complete, and some offensive words are left out on purpose. If a common word is missing, send it to us and we'll look at it for the next update.

Can I play in Norwegian?

Yes. Choose the board language when you start a game. The app language can be changed separately in Settings.

Free and Plus

What's free?

Free, with no ads: the Daily Arc, practice against the bot on Easy and Medium, pass & play, the tutorial and the first 20 stages of the Climb. You get one free hint in each game against the bot.

Wordarc Plus (one payment) unlocks the rest: stages 21–70 of the Climb, the hard bot and unlimited hints against the bot.

There are no ads in Wordarc, in the free version or with Plus. Not between games, not for hints, not anywhere.

I bought Plus but it's not active

Open Settings in Wordarc → Wordarc Plus → Restore purchases. Make sure Google Play is signed in with the same Google account you used to buy it. If the payment is still pending (for example a cash payment or a purchase waiting for a parent's approval), Plus turns on as soon as it's completed.

I have Plus on my iPhone

Google Play and the App Store are separate stores, so a purchase in one can't be restored in the other. Plus works on every Android device with the same Google account, and on every iPhone with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Within 48 hours you can request a refund yourself in Google Play. After that, see Google Play's refund help, or write to us.

Online games

Can I play online on Android?

Not yet. Online play is coming, with iPhone and Android in the same matches. Until then you can play the bot, pass & play, the Daily Arc and the Climb.

Data

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Your progress is stored on your phone. If Android backup is on, it comes with you when you restore a backup on the new phone. Plus can always be restored from Google Play.

What do you know about me?

Nothing. Wordarc has no accounts and collects no data. Read the privacy policy for details.
