How to play

Easy to learn in a minute. Hard to stop playing.

Make a wordDrag across neighbouring letters next to your bridge. Every word you play builds it higher.
Climb through the fogYou only see letters close to your bridge. Long words open up more of the board.
Steal and stormWords of six letters or more can steal your opponent's tiles. Seven or more send a storm that knocks their top tile loose.
Hold the topReach the crown at the top of the arc and hold it for one round to win.
