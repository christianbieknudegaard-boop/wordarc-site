Support

We read every message and usually reply within a day or two.

Write to hello@wordarc.app. If something went wrong, tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Playing

How do I win?

Build a bridge of tiles from your foot of the arc to the crown at the top. Reach the top and hold it for one round. If your opponent can't knock you off in their turn, you win.

Which letters can I use?

A word must start next to your bridge, and each letter must touch the one before it. Letters already in your bridge can be reused. Through the fog you only see letters close to your tiles, so longer words open up more of the board.

How do I steal tiles?

A word of six letters or more may pass through your opponent's tiles and take them over. Shorter words can't go through their tiles.

What is a storm?

Play a word of seven letters or more and it sends a storm at your opponent: their highest unlocked tile is knocked loose, and the free letters along their bridge are shuffled. Each player can send two storms per game.

A word I know wasn't accepted

Wordarc uses large word lists for each language, but no list is complete, and some offensive words are left out on purpose. If a common word is missing, send it to us and we'll look at it for the next update.

Can I play in Norwegian?

Yes. Choose the board language when you start a game. The app language can be changed separately in Settings.

Online games

I can't play online

Online play uses Game Center. Open the Settings app → Game Center and make sure you are signed in. Also check that multiplayer games aren't blocked under Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → Game Center.

How do I play with a friend?

Tap “Play online” and invite a friend through Game Center. You take turns whenever it suits you, and Game Center tells you when it's your move.

Why can I only have 5 games at once?

Free players can have five online games running at the same time. Wordarc Plus removes the limit.

Wordarc Plus

What does Plus include?

Unlimited online games, the whole Climb (stages 21–50), the hard bot, hints against the bot, statistics and game history, the Daily Arc archive, and extra colour themes, tiles and frames. It is a single payment, not a subscription, and never gives an edge against other players.

I bought Plus but it's not active

Open Settings in Wordarc and tap “Restore purchases”. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. If the purchase is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), Plus turns on as soon as it's approved.

Can my family use Plus?

If Family Sharing is turned on for the purchase, family members can restore Plus on their own devices.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Data

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Your stats and progress are stored on your iPhone and move with it when you restore from an iCloud or computer backup. Online games are kept by Game Center and follow your Apple Account. Plus can always be restored.

What do you know about me?

Nothing. Wordarc has no accounts and collects no data. Read the privacy policy for details.
